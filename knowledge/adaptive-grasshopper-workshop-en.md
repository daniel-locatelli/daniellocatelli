# Adaptive Grasshopper Workshop

Description: In addition to learning the basics of Grasshopper for Rhinoceros, participants were able to decide the direction of the course by choosing which strategy they would like to delve into among several others already well studied in the area of computational design: in this case, they chose "Exoskeleton.”
Category: Workshop
Location: Barra do Bugres
Place: UNEMAT Barra do Bugres
Date: June 2018 – June 2018

In addition to learning the basics of Grasshopper for Rhinoceros, participants were able to decide the direction of the course by choosing which strategy they would like to delve into among several others already well studied in the area of computational design: in this case, they chose "Exoskeleton.”
