## Building Across Scales: A Robotic Timber Fabrication System for On-Site Press Gluing

The research proposes a heterogeneous multi-scalar robotic construction system to further automate on-site timber construction. Specifically, it presents the next step in the automation of on-site gluing through the introduction of a custom robotic clamping device for the on-site pressuring of timber elements. At the core of the research lies the development of the clamping robot as part of a larger robotic construction team, including an industrial robot and crane in co-design with the material and building system.
