# Emotional Design and Technology

Description: A talk at MOVELPAR 2019, organized by SEBRAE, on how emotional design and technology intersect in contemporary furniture and product design.
Tags: Emotional Design, Technology, Furniture Design, Computational Design
Category: Talk
Authors: Daniel Nunes Locatelli
Location: Arapongas
Place: MOVELPAR
Date: March 2019
Event: MOVELPAR 2019
Language: Portuguese

A talk at MOVELPAR 2019, one of the largest furniture fairs in Latin America, organized by SEBRAE Talks. The presentation explored how emotional design and technology intersect in contemporary furniture and product design, demonstrating how computational tools can enhance the emotional connection between users and designed objects.
