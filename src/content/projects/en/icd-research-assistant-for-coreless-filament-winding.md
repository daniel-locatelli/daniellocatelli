At the moment there's no reliable computational tool for the development of coreless filament winding projects.

During manufacturing, as the robotic arm intertwines the fibres in a kind of three-dimensional weaving, the relationship of these fibres becomes very complex and difficult to predict.

Here I helped Christoph Schlopschnat in his investigation of computational methods to predict how the interaction of carbon fibres occurs.

I was responsible for the preparation of the resin, some small-scale physical tests and photogrammetry. What became more marked, however, was the toxicity of the resin, which requires good protection and preparation of the environment.
