As a research assistant, I helped Marta Gil Pérez with the load tests of the ITECH 2020 class pavilion.

In this work, the main thing I got from it was about the relationship between computer simulation, physical experiments, and the researcher's intuition.

Cutting-edge research like this does not yet have a reliable method of structural analysis through computer simulation. To achieve a safe structure, it is necessary to do these physical tests on a real scale.

In addition, Marta's experience and intuition with similar structures were fundamental to understanding what adjustments should be made to the project.
