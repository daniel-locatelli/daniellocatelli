  ### The big picture · 3 of 4

  ## Skills as deliverables, for *property-based development*

  - A skill is the container of knowledge: what to build, how, and how to check it
  - **Properties** are the contract every implementation has to pass, in any language
  - Implemented in Grasshopper first, validated, then made language-agnostic
notes: "Instead of a Grasshopper plugin, ship composable agent skills with a property-first specification. Code is cheap, so we do not have to commit to one language: the same skill can be built in Grasshopper, Revit, Cadwork or SEMA, and the properties are what every implementation must pass. Open conditions: defensible (evidence a skill works), demonstrable (one chain a reader can run), durable (plugins rot with Rhino versions, skills rot against models)."
